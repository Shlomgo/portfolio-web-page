Why I like this one

This is a scarce layout — four bedrooms and two full baths with real proportions across 1,699 square feet, not the shrunk-down fourth bedroom you usually see when a builder squeezes in an extra room. The kitchen is genuinely spacious, and the owner's suite and great room are both large enough to feel like a family actually lives there, not just visits.

Lennar is the second-largest homebuilder in the country and a Fortune 500 company. I've worked with them enough times now to know what I'm getting: consistent quality, a real warranty, and a company that isn't going anywhere.

One thing to plan around
Bad timing, on purpose fix

Construction finishes in the second half of November — right at the start of the slowest leasing season of the year. That's a real downside, not a footnote.

My recommendation: sign the first tenant to an 18-month lease instead of the usual 12. That pushes the lease-end into May, which means every renewal after the first one lands in high season instead of low season. It's a first-tenant problem only — after that, the timing fixes itself.

In low season it may take longer than the 30-day estimate, or require a small discount to get someone in the door faster rather than let the home sit empty.

The submarket

Benton is a Little Rock suburb with an easy commute into the city and schools with a strong local reputation. Most tenants here either commute into Little Rock or work in the area's growing retail corridor.

This part of the metro is growing faster than the Little Rock area as a whole, and that trend is expected to continue.
