Why I like this one

This is a well-thought-out three-bedroom layout — 1,355 square feet without a lot of wasted space. I especially like that the dining room has its own nook rather than being squeezed into the living room, while the kitchen remains open to the main living area. The bedrooms are a reasonable size, and the covered patio gives the house a proper place to sit outside. Having the appliances and fencing already included means fewer things to organize before renting it.

Lennar is the second-largest homebuilder in the U.S. by annual home closings and a Fortune 500 company. I've worked with them enough times now to know what I'm getting: consistent quality, a real warranty, and a company that isn't going to disappear.

The underwriting above is pulled from the same spreadsheet row as the portfolio table, so the assumptions stay synchronized as the proposal changes. I have included $2,200 for blinds and a glass shower door — not must-haves, but improvements I recommend.

One thing to plan around
A sloped yard, but plenty of usable space

A sizeable part of the fenced backyard slopes upward. That's a real downside, not a footnote: it limits how someone might use the yard and can make lawn maintenance less convenient.

What makes me comfortable with it is that there is still a good-sized flat section that tenants can actually enjoy. The covered patio is also useful outdoor space regardless of the slope.

Before purchasing, I would make sure the grading and drainage are doing their job and that maintaining the sloped part of the lawn won't become a problem.

The submarket

Benton is a Little Rock suburb with an easy commute into the city and schools with a strong local reputation. Most tenants here either commute into Little Rock or work in the area's growing retail corridor.

This part of the metro is growing faster than the Little Rock area as a whole, and that trend is expected to continue.
