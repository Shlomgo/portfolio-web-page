Why I like this one

The house itself feels unusually bright and spacious for an entry-level three-bedroom new build. The living room, kitchen and dining area read as one open central space instead of three small rooms, while the owner’s suite is separated from the secondary bedrooms. That combination should work well for a family renter and also makes the home feel more substantial than the bedroom count suggests.

The lot is another major reason I like this specific home. It is one of the larger lots in Midland Estates, with a very big front lawn and no house directly behind it, so the property feels unusually open for new construction in this price range.

Several rent-ready items are included by the builder.

The deal includes a refrigerator, washer, dryer and a fence. Those are items an investor would normally have to buy or install after closing, so having them included reduces the immediate setup work and makes the home more turnkey for the first tenant.

Alexander is a small community on the southwest side of the Little Rock metropolitan area. This address is in Saline County, just beyond Bryant along the I-30 corridor. That location gives tenants a straightforward commute into Little Rock while keeping them close to the shopping, restaurants, services and family-oriented suburban growth around Bryant and Benton.

A lot of the same reasons people choose Benton and Bryant apply here: more house and yard for the money than they would typically get closer to downtown, a suburban setting, access to a school district with a strong local reputation, and a direct interstate connection to the larger Little Rock employment base. I-30 is the key piece of the location story; it is the main commuter spine linking Benton, Bryant, Alexander and Little Rock.

Midland Estates itself is new construction by Lennar, with a mix of three- and four-bedroom single-family homes. For this investment, I like that the location is easy to explain to a renter: it is suburban, school-oriented and still close enough to Little Rock for a normal daily commute.
